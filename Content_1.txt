Once upon a time, there was a man named Alex who worked in a factory full of machines. Every day, he used machines to complete his work faster and more efficiently.

One day, the machines suddenly stopped working. Everyone panicked, but Alex remained calm. He carefully examined the machines, identified the problem, and repaired them.

His manager praised him and said, "Machines are powerful, but they need humans to guide them."

Alex smiled and replied, "Machines can do many things, but human creativity, intelligence, and problem-solving make them truly useful."

From that day forward, Alex and the machines worked together to make the factory more productive.

**Moral:** Machines can make our lives easier, but human intelligence and creativity give them purpose.
